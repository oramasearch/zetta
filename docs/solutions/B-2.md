# B-2 Employee Attrition (aggregate-first)

B-2 estimates workforce attrition risk for teams, roles, or tenure bands and
publishes aggregate drivers to a named people leader. It never creates an
employee risk flag or sends an individual score to a manager. The module is
runnable: `create_solution` builds its native M-1 and M-7 stages, while
`run_solution` executes the snapshot, aggregate scoring, policy, permanent
holdout, people-leader delivery, ledger, monitor, driver report, and ROI
readout.

The catalogue promotion condition remains product guidance: offer B-2 only
where the workforce is large enough, a people leader owns the output, and
legal or works-council approval exists. It does not change engineering
runnability.

## Data contract

The landed source contains one row per protected cohort, never one row per
employee.

| Role | Required | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| group | yes | Stable team, role, or tenure-band key used as the modeling entity |
| group size | yes | Numeric `group_size`; every row must meet the configured minimum of at least five |
| label | yes | Aggregate attrition outcome for the cohort |
| time | yes | Workforce snapshot cutoff and outcome window |
| feature | yes | Aggregate workforce measures used for risk and driver estimation |
| owner | yes | Named people leader receiving the aggregate report |
| approval | yes | Recorded legal or works-council approval |

The default individual-output blocklist is `employee_id`, `person_id`,
`worker_id`, `individual_score`, and `employee_score`. A snapshot containing
one of those fields is refused before methodology execution. Protected
attributes are named in the fairness review but cannot be model features.

## Levels

| Level | Native stages | Behavior |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | M-1, M-7 | Aggregate team risk, ranked drivers, permanent holdout, people-leader report, and readout |
| 2 | M-1, M-7 | Adds role-band comparison under the same output protections |
| 3 | M-1, M-7 | Adds tenure-band anomaly context without changing the aggregate unit |
| 4 | M-1, M-7 | Adds an approval-gated organizational-intervention review |

All levels call both registered methodology callbacks. M-1 writes
`workforce_results.aggregate_attrition_risk_<run>` with `group_key` and
`attrition_risk`; that table is the module's real `decision_source`. M-7 adds
cohort-level anomaly context. Neither stage has an employee identity.

## Executable defaults

The elevated tier begins at 0.60 and creates an A2
`people_leader_review`. The watch tier begins at 0.35 and creates an A2
`aggregate_risk_report`. The baseline tier records A0 `no_action`. An optional
`organizational_intervention_review` is A1. Actions have a seven-day SLA, a
rolling limit of one per 30 days, and a lifetime cap of four.

Eligibility requires all of the following:

- `group_size >= 5`;
- `people_leader_authorized == true`;
- `fairness_review_passed == true`;
- `legal_approval_recorded == true`;
- `individual_level_output == false`.

Restricted cohorts and any row requesting individual output are excluded. The
starter budget is zero and the kill switch is off. Elevated, watch, and
baseline tiers each have a deterministic 10% permanent holdout. The remaining
groups enter the `people_leader_review` treatment cell. Measurement records the
named `attrition_reduction` outcome per group over 90 days.

The five monitor values are small-group outputs, individual-score outputs,
fairness-review failures, approval-gate failures, and action-execution rate.
The first four must remain zero; the execution-rate minimum is 0.95. These
thresholds are editable starter settings, not customer calibration or return
claims.

## Required approvals

`sources.correctness` must record a completed fairness review with an owner,
protected cohorts, and review metrics. It must also record an approval with:

- `approval_recorded: true`;
- `approval_kind: legal` or `works_council`;
- nonempty `approval_record_id`, `approved_by`, and `approved_at`.

Missing evidence is a create-time refusal. The same immutable profile remains
bound to the graph for every run. Policy gates are revalidated at run time, so
a later policy version cannot remove the group-size, fairness, approval, or
aggregate-only rules.

## Run it

1. Land a cohort table containing only aggregate rows and create a project.
2. Call `get_solution` with `{"solution":"B-2"}` and choose level 1–4.
3. Call `create_solution` with M-1 and M-7 hints, the snapshot and dataset
   contracts, the people-leader delivery owner, ROI economics, and the required
   `sources.correctness` evidence.
4. Call `run_solution`, then poll `get_run`.
5. Read the aggregate decision table, the M-1 workforce-results table,
   experiment assignments, action ledger, monitor, and aggregate driver report.
6. Record `attrition_reduction` outcomes against the ledger actions and call
   `get_roi_readout` after the outcome window closes.

Action payloads contain only the group key, group dimension, group size,
aggregate attrition risk, dominant driver, approval record, and decision
metadata. The destination owner is always the people leader recorded in the
foundation; source-row ownership cannot redirect delivery.

## Correctness refusals

`create_solution` and `run_solution` execute the same composed gate. Each
condition below refuses its bad case:

1. Every output stage enforces the same minimum group size, and the minimum
   cannot be below five.
2. The modeling identity is the cohort key, all stages declare
   `aggregate_only`, prohibited individual columns cannot be features, and the
   decision source is M-1's `group_key` / `attrition_risk` table.
3. A completed, owned fairness review names protected cohorts and review
   metrics; protected attributes cannot be selected as features.
4. A legal or works-council approval has a record identifier, approver, and
   timestamp before execution.
5. Output is owned by a named `people_leader`; individual-manager delivery is
   false.

The journey uses 180 synthetic cohort rows plus a future row that the snapshot
excludes. It proves protected holdouts receive no action, every published row
meets the group-size minimum, no individual field reaches a result or action
payload, and the ROI readout recovers the planted aggregate effect inside its
confidence interval.
