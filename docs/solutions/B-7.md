# B-7 Credit Underwriting & Limit Management

B-7 turns matured repayment evidence into governed credit-risk scores,
examiner-readable reason codes, underwriter tasks, and bounded credit or limit
recommendations. It is runnable through `create_solution` and `run_solution`.
Native M-1 produces applicant-level credit risk and explanations; M-17 measures
uncertainty coverage on a disjoint holdout.

Engineering readiness does not remove the catalogue promotion condition. The
module may be offered to customers only after a lender's model-risk team
co-signs and exercises the complete validation pipeline.

## Data contract

| Role | Required | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| entity | yes | Stable applicant, account, or facility identifier |
| label | yes | Matured repayment or default outcome |
| time | yes | Application cutoff and performance-window boundary |
| feature | yes | Verified point-in-time application and bureau inputs |
| group | yes | Portfolio group for M-17 partial pooling and coverage validation |
| eligibility | yes | Identity, income, product, and policy eligibility evidence |
| protected cohort | yes | Evaluation-only fair-lending cohorts, never model features |
| reason | adverse outcomes | Ranked adverse-action reason codes persisted with the decision |
| owner | yes | Accountable underwriter, credit team, model-risk owner, and fairness owner |

The default model features are ordinary numeric underwriting inputs such as
income, debt ratio, and utilization. Race, ethnicity, sex, gender, age,
disability, religion, and national origin are blocked from model features.
Evaluation-only cohort columns support approval-rate and false-negative-rate
review.

## Levels

| Level | Native stages | Behavior |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | M-1, M-17 | Credit risk, reason codes, uncertainty validation, holdout, and underwriter tasks |
| 2 | M-1, M-17 | Level 1 plus governed limit review |
| 3 | M-1, M-17 | Level 2 plus uncertainty-aware escalation |
| 4 | M-1, M-17 | Level 3 plus portfolio limit management |

Every level invokes both registered methodology callbacks. The primary
decision source is the M-1 `Publish credit risk scores` output in
`credit_results`, keyed by `applicant` with numeric `credit_risk`.

## Policy, experiment, and measurement

The editable starter policy has elevated-risk, review, and eligible tiers at
0.7, 0.4, and 0.0. Their defaults are an underwriter task, a limit-review task,
and credit approval. Approval within a requested limit of 5,000 is A2; a larger
request requires A1 approval. Limit increases are A1. Decline and lower-limit
recommendations are always A0, are never selected directly by a score tier,
and never authorize automated action.

Eligibility, income verification, model-validation approval, fairness-review
approval, and reason-code readiness must all be true. Fraud holds, bankruptcy
holds, and manual-only flags block delivery. The action cap is one decision per
entity. Every tier has a deterministic 10% protected holdout. The named outcome
is `performing_account` per application over 90 days.

The monitor records automated adverse decisions, missing adverse-action
reasons, model-validation approval failures, fairness-review failures,
approval-rate gap, and action-execution rate. Delivered payloads include the
applicant, proposed action, requested limit, decision cell, reason codes,
model-risk owner, fairness owner, and the A0 adverse-authority marker.

## Run it

1. Land a point-in-time application table and create a project.
2. Call `get_solution` with `{"solution":"B-7"}` and select level 1–4.
3. Call `create_solution` with snapshot, dataset, delivery, and ROI foundation
   settings. Set M-17's entity, label, group, nominal coverage, and holdout
   hints under `sources.M-17`.
4. Put validation and fairness ownership under `sources.correctness`.
5. Call `run_solution`, poll `get_run`, then read the credit score table,
   decision table, policy, experiment, action ledger, and monitor.
6. Record `performing_account` against ledger actions and read the effect with
   `get_roi_readout` after the outcome window.

## Refusals

The composed gate rejects B-7 when any of these conditions is false:

1. Every adverse action requires at least one persisted TreeSHAP reason code.
2. Development, independent validation, approval, and monitoring are present;
   the named model-risk owner co-signs out-of-time, calibration, and M-17
   coverage review.
3. An owned fair-lending review has passed, includes protected cohorts,
   approval and false-negative metrics, and excludes protected attributes from
   model features.
4. Score tiers do not select adverse outcomes; every decline or adverse limit
   recommendation remains A0 human-only.
5. Every level contains M-1 and M-17, and M-1 publishes the real applicant and
   credit-risk columns.

Runtime policy validation repeats the authority and required-evidence checks.
It also refuses any adverse decision without reason codes or any adverse
decision that becomes actionable. The journey plants a 0.14
performing-account effect, proves holdout suppression and A0 adverse handling,
and recovers that effect inside its confidence interval.
