# B-1 Dynamic Pricing & Discount Governance

B-1 produces price or discount recommendations inside explicit gross-margin
and authority limits. It can create review tasks, run controlled price
experiments, and publish approved price changes. A recommendation outside the
hard authority band is blocked. A level-3 or level-4 change above the automatic
band becomes A1 pending approval and is never delivered automatically.

The catalogue entry is runnable. Its customer-offering condition remains:
promote when a target account has identifiable price variation or permits
controlled price experiments. That commercial condition does not change
engineering readiness.

## Data by role

| Role | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| entity | stable product, customer, and market key |
| treatment | randomized or otherwise identifiable price/discount assignment |
| label | demand response and realized net margin after treatment |
| time | historical demand cutoff, competitor-price observation time, treatment time, and outcome time |
| price | current and recommended prices available at decision time |
| margin | gross margin used by the policy floor |
| authority | absolute distance from the current price, automatic limit, and hard limit |

M-4 reads the bounded historical table and publishes its real `forecast`
output. The default decision source is `series` / `point_forecast`; it does not
use a churn score table. M-6 estimates the treatment effect from randomized or
otherwise identifiable variation. M-16 solves and independently verifies the
declared pricing constraints. M-6 and M-16 read the point-in-time decision
snapshot.

## Levels

| Level | Behavior | Action authority |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | forecast and governed price recommendation | `price_recommendation`, A0 |
| 2 | add owned pricing-review tasks | `pricing_review_task`, A2 |
| 3 | add controlled price experiments with holdout | `controlled_price_experiment`, A2 inside the automatic band and A1 above it |
| 4 | publish approved price changes | `publish_price_change`, A2 inside the automatic band and A1 above it |

All four levels execute M-4, M-6, and M-16. Every level uses per-tier holdouts.
The default measurement is treated-minus-holdout net margin over 30 days.

## Policy and actions

The executable template requires `pricing_eligible` and a fresh current price,
enforces `gross_margin >= 0.25`, and blocks
`authority_distance > 0.15`. Manual-only and legal-hold rows are excluded.
Controlled experiments and published changes carry
`approval_field: authority_distance` with `approval_above: 0.05`.

Delivered payloads contain the product-market key, current and recommended
prices, gross margin, authority distance, estimated M-6 effect, decision cell,
policy action, and action-ledger identifiers. Protected holdouts, A0 records,
blocked rows, and pending approvals produce ledger evidence but no connector
request.

Guardrails observe margin-floor violations, hard authority-band violations,
unapproved price changes, price-feature leakage, and action execution.

## Correctness refusals

The composed gate refuses:

- price-effect estimation without randomized experiments or another declared
  source of identifiable variation;
- a policy that removes or weakens the gross-margin floor, hard authority
  limit, or approval threshold;
- competitor-price observations after the decision time, future own or
  competitor prices, future discounts, or post-offer demand in features;
- a level missing M-4, M-6, M-16, its registered action, or the level-4 kill
  switch.

The policy limits are also checked immediately before actions. Editing a stored
policy cannot remove the margin, hard-band, or approval controls.

## Run

1. Land historical demand with the roles above and create a project.
2. Call `get_solution` with `{"solution":"B-1"}` and select level 1–4.
3. Call `create_solution` with M-4 history columns, M-6 treatment/outcome
   columns, `sources.correctness` identification evidence, and the standard
   snapshot, dataset, delivery, and ROI foundation.
4. Read the policy and experiment, then call `run_solution` and poll `get_run`.
5. Read decisions and the ledger. Record matured `net_margin` outcomes against
   action IDs and call `get_roi_readout`.

`SolutionJourney.DynamicPricingEnforcesBandsAndRecoversPlantedEffect` runs
level 4 on partitioned synthetic history. It proves the margin floor and hard
band block their bad rows, over-automatic-band changes remain pending approval,
holdouts never reach the connector, all five guardrails are observed, and the
readout recovers a planted 0.40 net-margin effect inside its confidence
interval.
