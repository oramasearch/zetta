# Vision — Intelligence Velocity

**Orama Zetta turns a question into running, deployed intelligence.** The
product name is the metric: **Intelligence Velocity** — the time from intent
to deployed, running, correct intelligence — compressed from quarters to
minutes, **for big thinkers**: the people whose ideas outrun their cycle time.

## The one flow

**Ingest** (plain language, or an artifact you already have — an n8n workflow,
a SQL script, a notebook) → **Architect** (compile a correct execution graph,
validated before it runs) → **Deploy** (one button, onto infrastructure you
control) → **Run + learn** (answers with lineage; it sharpens with what it
sees).

## What we are

**Auto-ANI, not AGI**: automated, artificial *narrow* intelligence —
production-grade, specialized to one job and your data, optimized for the
three things that decide whether AI ever ships: **cost, governance, and
personalized accuracy**. The engine underneath is the world's first
**adaptive database** — one system that is simultaneously the control-plane
spine and the data/compute substrate (replicated store + indexes +
compute-near-data + tiered storage), running as a **true hybrid** equal-peer
cluster across any vendor, architecture, and cloud.

## The three convictions

1. **It learns from your data.** If you're not training on your data, you're
   not fully using AI — prompting a rented general model is renting a
   confident guess. Zetta trains models (often ensembles) on the customer's
   own data, inside their walls.
2. **Correct by construction.** Generating a workflow is easy; generating a
   *correct* one is the product — schema/type checks, leakage and
   point-in-time checks, entity resolution, a gate that blocks deploy.
3. **Governed self-serve.** Governance is the precondition for safe
   self-service: identity-scoped, audited, consent-gated, no egress.

## The name

*Zetta* — a zettabyte is one billion terabytes. Build for the ceiling and the
everyday problems come for free.
